## Methodology note

Nearly all direct-fetch attempts today against primary news domains (Times of Israel, Haaretz, Manchester University, Smithsonian, Live Science, ScienceAlert, Big Think, Armstrong Institute, arXiv.org) returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`, consistent with prior sessions. Verification relied on convergent WebSearch synthesis across multiple independent outlets (JPost, Times of Israel, Haaretz, Sci.News, Archaeology Magazine, Biblical Archaeology Society, and, where available, publisher/journal abstract text) plus direct comparison against the existing `data.json` entry text, rather than single-source primary-document retrieval. Where a candidate's date or novelty could not be pinned down with full confidence, it was resolved conservatively — treated as not-new or already-covered rather than risking inclusion of stale or duplicate material.
