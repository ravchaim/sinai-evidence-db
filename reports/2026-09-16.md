## New Items

TITLE: The City of David's Connected First Temple Wall and the "Tsafan" Bulla
CATEGORY: history_corrob
EXPLANATION: In March 2026, the Israel Antiquities Authority, excavating in Jerusalem's City of David National Park on the eastern slope just south of the Gihon Spring, exposed a previously missing stretch of Jerusalem's First Temple-period fortification wall, physically connecting a ~100-foot section Kathleen Kenyon had excavated there in the 1960s to a ~300-foot section Yigal Shiloh excavated roughly a decade later. This confirms the two earlier finds were part of a single continuous city wall (rather than the disputed fragments some had suggested) — the very wall the Babylonian army under Nebuchadnezzar would have confronted before Jerusalem's destruction in 586 BCE. Immediately adjacent to the wall, excavators recovered a cache of artifacts from the city's final years: jar handles bearing "rosette" stamp impressions (an administrative marking associated with the late Kingdom of Judah), LMLK seal impressions, a Babylonian stone stamp seal depicting a worshiper before symbols of the gods Marduk and Nabu, and a clay bulla (seal impression) inscribed with the Judahite personal name "Tsafan." Taken together, the reconnected wall and the destruction-era artifacts beside it offer physical corroboration of Jerusalem's fortified state and its fall as narrated in 2 Kings 25 and Jeremiah 39, complementing this project's existing entries on other City of David destruction-layer finds (e.g., the Givati Parking Lot burnt beams). Note: this is a newly announced excavation result reported by the excavating body and multiple Israeli outlets; it has not yet appeared in a peer-reviewed publication, so it should be treated with the same provisional caution appropriate to any preliminary excavation announcement pending formal publication.
SOURCE: Israel Antiquities Authority / City of David Foundation excavation announcement, March 2026, corroborated by independent reporting in JNS, Ynet, Times of Israel, and Tazpit Press Service (TPS)
URL: https://cityofdavid.org.il/en/excavations-have-exposed-the-missing-section-of-the-city-wall-of-jerusalem-eng/
