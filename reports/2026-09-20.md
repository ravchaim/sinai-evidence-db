## Methodology note

Several primary-source and reference domains (en.wikipedia.org, en.huji.ac.il, ancientneareasttoday.substack.com) returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED` on direct WebFetch today, as in prior sessions; verification for items discussed above relied on convergent WebSearch results (Jerusalem Post, Times of Israel, Biblical Archaeology Society, Haaretz, JJAR listings, and, where available, publisher/journal abstract text) rather than direct primary-document retrieval in every case. Where a candidate's novelty or date could not be confirmed with confidence (e.g., precise 2026 vs. earlier-year publication), it was resolved conservatively — treated as not-new rather than risking inclusion of stale material as fresh.
