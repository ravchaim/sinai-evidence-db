TITLE: The Tel Hadid Mother-of-Pearl Seal — Assyrian-Era Moon-God Imagery at a Site Named in Ezra-Nehemiah

CATEGORY: archaeology

EXPLANATION: A 2026 peer-reviewed article in the journal *Levant* (Koch, Leibin-Graiver, Bar-Yosef Mayer, Ranzer, Wrathall, Myers, and Parker, "A unique mother-of-pearl stamp seal from Late Iron Age Tel Ḥadid, Israel") describes a small 7th-century BCE stamp seal excavated at Tel Hadid in central Israel, made from mother-of-pearl sourced from *Pinctada margaritifera* (the black-lipped pearl oyster), a species native to the Indo-Pacific — a material previously undocumented in the southern Levantine stamp-seal corpus. Its engraved iconography is identified by the researchers as imagery associated with the moon god of Harran, a Mesopotamian deity whose cult symbol (a crescent mounted on a standard) is attested widely across the Neo-Assyrian Empire in the 8th–7th centuries BCE. Tel Hadid is the well-established archaeological identification of biblical Hadid, named three times in Ezra 2:33, Nehemiah 7:37, and Nehemiah 11:34 in connection with the return from Babylonian exile; the site has separately yielded cuneiform tablets documenting land transactions of foreign deportees settled there, consistent with the Assyrian resettlement policy described in 2 Kings 17:24. The seal itself does not mention Israel, Judah, or any biblical figure — its evidentiary value for this project is as physical confirmation, at a specific site independently identified by name in the biblical text, of exactly the two things 2 Kings 17 and the broader Assyrian-period record describe for this region: (1) the importation of foreign, non-Israelite religious cults into formerly Israelite territory under Assyrian administration, and (2) that territory's participation in long-distance imperial trade networks. This is incremental corroborating material culture, not a discovery that independently proves any biblical claim, and should be read as such.

SOURCE: I. Koch, D. Leibin-Graiver, D. E. Bar-Yosef Mayer, N. Ranzer, A. Wrathall, G. Myers, and J. Parker, "A unique mother-of-pearl stamp seal from Late Iron Age Tel Ḥadid, Israel," *Levant* (2026), DOI: 10.1080/00758914.2026.2663758

URL: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00758914.2026.2663758
