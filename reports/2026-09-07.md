## New Item

TITLE: New Ancient DNA from the Megiddo VII Excavation Volume
CATEGORY: genetics
EXPLANATION: A chapter by Lily Agranat-Tamir, Benjamin Yakir, Shai Carmi, and Liran Carmel ("DNA of Individuals from Tomb 16/H/50 and Burial 16/H/45," pp. 235–239) appears in *Megiddo VII: The Shmunis Excavations of a Monumental Middle Bronze Tomb and Its Environs* (eds. Matthew J. Adams, Melissa S. Cradic, and Israel Finkelstein; Eisenbrauns/Penn State University Press, 2025), the official excavation report for a monumental Middle Bronze III elite tomb and an adjacent burial at Tel Megiddo — a site named repeatedly in the Tanach (e.g. Joshua 12:21, Judges 5:19, 1 Kings 9:15, 2 Kings 23:29). The chapter presents new whole-genome ancient DNA from 35 individuals spanning roughly 1,200 years (Intermediate Bronze Age through Iron Age), including the tomb's occupants. Reported findings include that many of the sampled individuals were biologically related to one another (including two brothers with virtually identical ancestry), that Middle-to-Late Bronze Age Megiddo individuals carry a mixture of local Levantine ("Canaanite") ancestry with an additional component related to the Caucasus/Zagros region, and that combined isotopic and genetic evidence suggests at least some individuals of immigrant ancestry were established locally within a generation or two. This adds new, site-specific genomic data point to the same broad evidentiary category as the database's existing "Canaanite Genetic Continuity (Haber et al.)" entry — population-genetic characterization of Bronze/Iron Age Levantine society — but from a different site, a different and more recent (2025) dataset, and with distinct findings about kinship and migrant integration at a major biblical-period city on the eve of and during the Israelite highland settlement. It bears modestly on the project's themes as a data point about the demographic composition of Canaanite society at a named biblical site; it does not itself address Israelite origins or ethnicity directly, and should not be overstated as such.
SOURCE: Agranat-Tamir, L., Yakir, B., Carmi, S., & Carmel, L., "DNA of Individuals from Tomb 16/H/50 and Burial 16/H/45," in M.J. Adams, M.S. Cradic, & I. Finkelstein (eds.), *Megiddo VII: The Shmunis Excavations of a Monumental Middle Bronze Tomb and Its Environs*, Eisenbrauns, 2025, pp. 235–239.
URL: https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/dna-of-individuals-from-tomb-16h50-and-burial-16h45/
